University Communications serves as a creative conduit for sharing the passion, progress, people and history that tell the story of Grand Valley State University.

This annual report is designed to “show our work” in qualitative and quantitative ways while demonstrating how our award-winning team of communication professionals serves our community by sharing Laker stories with audiences both broad and targeted.

The report also sets the bar for our team as we seek to improve, refine and adapt to new opportunities and challenges.

As this report will show, the team reached millions of people through creative, passionate storytelling to drive recruitment, improve retention and show how special it is to be a Laker for a Lifetime.

Whether in print via GVMagazine, online in GVNext and Forum, visual storytelling with video and photography, at Lubbers Stadium for a home football game, or on one of our many social media channels, UComm’s team of talented professionals embraced the GVSU mission of empowering learners in their pursuits, professions and purpose.

UComm is also dedicated to bringing our diverse GVSU communications community closer together through its leadership of the University Communicator Network. In its third year, the UCN continued to provide professional development and networking opportunities that fostered learning and relationships among more than 250 talented professionals.

UComm will continue to meet the challenge of achieving the goals of Reach Higher Together, showing and telling the stories of the thousands of Lakers — past, present and future — who are turning “What If” into “What Is” in their communities and around the world.
