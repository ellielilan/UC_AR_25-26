Annual Report 2025 - 2026

Our Services & Expertise

UComm plays a central role in the Incident Management Team and works closely with GVSU’s Department of Public Safety to develop emergency communications strategy, safety messaging and GVPD social media.

Crisis communications

UComm serves as the primary conduit for interaction with members of the media through proactive outreach and reactive responses, generating earned media coverage, managing crisis and emergency situations, and hosting news conferences and credential management.

UComm provides media training to faculty and staff to help them prepare for interviews and provides talking points to senior leaders to react to current news.

– Cision, media impressions, placements, etc.

Media Relations

UComm assists the Office of the President with speeches, OpEds, podcasting, livestreaming, video production, social media, photography and design services, and other strategic guidance. One highlight was the inclusion of a book featuring the year’s top University Communications photographs, selected as a “Philly’s Faves” item and sold through the Laker Store.

Presidential Communications Assistance

In addition to creating content for GVSU’s channels, UComm provides consulting services to the broader GVSU communicator community on social media best practices. The team also continually monitors the broader social media ecosystem for reputational and risk management.

Social Media monitoring and Consulting

UComm employs and provides professional experience and internship opportunities to a team of students, including writers, photographers, videographers, social media interns and social media ambassadors.
